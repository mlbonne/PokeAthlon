## Stage art: Pokémon Stadium 2 (Super Smash Bros. Brawl)

`assets/stadium/brawl/*` is a BrawlBox rip of **Pokémon Stadium 2** from *Super Smash Bros. Brawl*.

- Models, textures and stage design © Nintendo / HAL Laboratory.
- Fan use only, non-commercial. This repository must stay private.
- No license is granted for these files; they are not covered by the project's licence.
- Converted and baked by `tools/stage/convert.ts` from a local `stage-assets/` rip (not committed).
- The score canvas, floor marks and procedural searchlights are original PokéAthlon code.

Floor variants (`floor-*.glb`), the big screen (`screen.glb`), sky (`sky.glb`), platforms and smoke
are all part of the same rip.
