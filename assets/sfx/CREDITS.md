## Sound effects: Pokémon Mystery Dungeon sounds and Pokémon cries

- **Move, battle, menu and fanfare sounds** (`pmd/`): from *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Origins*
  (<https://github.com/audinowho/DumpAsset>, `Content/Sound/`; software © Audino, free to copy and
  redistribute, see its README for full credits). The sounds are original *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon*
  audio by Spike Chunsoft / Nintendo. 87 files are used here.
- **Pokémon cries** (`cries/`, named by PokéAPI id): from PokeAPI's cries repository
  (<https://github.com/PokeAPI/cries>), which gathered them from Pokémon Showdown, Veekun and The
  Sounds Resource. The cries are © Nintendo / Creatures / GAME FREAK. 9 files are used here.

They're used for non-commercial fan purposes only, with attribution.
