## Move effects: Pokémon Mystery Dungeon effect sheets (PMDCollab RawAsset)

Source: <https://github.com/PMDCollab/RawAsset> (`Particle/`, `Beam/`), the effect art used by
*Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Origins* (<https://github.com/audinowho/DumpAsset>, software © Audino,
free to copy and redistribute; see its README for full credits).

- Most sheets are original *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon* effects by Spike Chunsoft / Nintendo. The
  "RSE" sets come from the Generation III games and the "Ranger" sets from *Pokémon Ranger*.
- Some are custom PMD: Origins effects (for example `Crab_Hammer_PMDO`).
- 85 files are used here (see `manifest.json` in this folder). They're used for non-commercial fan
  purposes only, with attribution. `@crater` and `@scorch` are drawn in code by PokéAthlon.
