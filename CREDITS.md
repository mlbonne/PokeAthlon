## Move effects: Pokémon Mystery Dungeon effect sheets (PMDCollab RawAsset)

Source: <https://github.com/PMDCollab/RawAsset> (`Particle/`, `Beam/`), the effect art used by
*Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Origins* (<https://github.com/audinowho/DumpAsset>, software © Audino,
free to copy and redistribute; see its README for full credits).

- Most sheets are original *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon* effects by Spike Chunsoft / Nintendo. The
  "RSE" sets come from the Generation III games and the "Ranger" sets from *Pokémon Ranger*.
- Some are custom PMD: Origins effects (for example `Crab_Hammer_PMDO`).
- 85 effect sheets plus the `Beam/Lightning` strip are used here (see `assets/fx/pmd/manifest.json`). They're used for non-commercial fan
  purposes only, with attribution. `@crater` and `@scorch` are drawn in code by PokéAthlon.

## Sound effects: Pokémon Mystery Dungeon sounds and Pokémon cries

- **Move, battle, menu and fanfare sounds** (`pmd/`): from *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Origins*
  (<https://github.com/audinowho/DumpAsset>, `Content/Sound/`; software © Audino, free to copy and
  redistribute, see its README for full credits). The sounds are original *Pokémon Mystery Dungeon*
  audio by Spike Chunsoft / Nintendo. 87 files are used here.
- **Pokémon cries** (`cries/`, named by PokéAPI id): from PokeAPI's cries repository
  (<https://github.com/PokeAPI/cries>), which gathered them from Pokémon Showdown, Veekun and The
  Sounds Resource. The cries are © Nintendo / Creatures / GAME FREAK. 9 files are used here.

They're used for non-commercial fan purposes only, with attribution.
